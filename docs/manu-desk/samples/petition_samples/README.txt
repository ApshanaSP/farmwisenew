Manu Desk sample petitions (made-up test data)

30 petitions, 33 images. Every person, phone number, Aadhaar number and case is invented.
Mobile numbers start with 90000 and Aadhaar numbers start with 9999, so they cannot belong to anyone.
Areas, wards, streets and pincodes are real Chennai places.

These are simulated photos. The writing comes from handwriting fonts, with each letter moved and bent, then
paper damage, room lighting and phone-camera effects added. Use them to test paper, lighting, layout, field
extraction and routing. They are NOT a measure of how well real handwriting can be read, so real pen-written
samples are still needed for that.

answers.csv opens in Excel with Tamil intact. ward_on_page is blank when the ward is not written on the paper;
ward_for_area is what a correct area lookup should find. mobile is blank when no number is written.
case_group marks the same person writing twice (same-person-A) and three people about one road (same-road-B).

Mix: 01-14 handwritten Tamil, 15-20 handwritten English, 21-24 printed with a signature,
25-27 very messy Tamil with English words, 28-30 two pages each.
