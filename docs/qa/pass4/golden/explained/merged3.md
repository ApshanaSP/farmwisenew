That pushes the last of the 22, the Tamil reservoir sentence (`golden-direct-22`), out of the list: one request removed, one added. The Tamil reservoir sentence is still recorded as an ordinary request, and its change is in the table above.
- The four follow-ups start from the cases screen of Adyar instead of from the ranking of severe incidents, so their bodies carry a different `stage` and their ids change: four removed, four added.
