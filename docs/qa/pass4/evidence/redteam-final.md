## What could not run, and why

- No model ran: the brief names no Groq run, so servers had no key. Injection and privacy through a live model are therefore not covered; the model path was tested with an obedient fake model at the server's gates only.
- News headlines and complaint titles come from the pinned snapshot, which other agents share; planting orders in it would change their data, so it was not done. A complaint filed now does not reach any staff view until the pipeline's next build (`lib/officer/queue-note.ts`), so it could not carry an injection either.
- The Confirm it's you emailed code and every real email: SMTP is off on test servers by the rules. The burst counts above are the codes made and the send attempts in the log.
- Jarvis's ears and voice (`/api/jarvis/hear`, `/api/jarvis/say`) answer 503 without a speech key, before reading the body, so their body limits and spoken lines through the voice could not be tested.
- Password-reset race with the right code: the code is never returned on a production build (by design), so it could not be used.
- Browser-side checks (the console's DANGER_BUTTON and `data-jarvis-never`) were read in code, not driven in a browser.
