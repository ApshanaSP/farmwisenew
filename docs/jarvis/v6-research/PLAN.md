# v6 plan: what to build, ranked (Phase 1)

Written 17 Nov 2026 from the four research notes in this folder (10 turn-taking, 20 voice-agent speed, 30 local speech, 40 news and web), the Phase 0 waterfall (`chennai-grievance-portal-main/eval/v6/latency/phase0-waterfall.md`) and the offline and browser probes logged in `docs/thunai/BUILD_LOG.md` (v6). **A figure here is either measured on this PC (says so, with its file) or read in a source (the research note that cites it); "inferred" marks my own reasoning.** Response time wins over correctness only where both cannot be had (the brief, section 1); no lever below trades correctness away.

## Where the time goes today (Phase 0, real providers, Edge's natural voices, from the true end of speech)

See the waterfall file for every stage with n, p50 and p90. The order of the big items, measured:

1. **Edge's natural voice starting cold** (speak() handed the answer → the voice's `start` event): the single largest piece on answered turns (see the waterfall's "TTS" and "voice start delay" rows). Measured apart (BUILD_LOG 12:05-12:20): 543-1,560 ms cold (Neerja), 585-1,451 ms (Pallavi); 145-269 ms when a silent utterance went out at least 300 ms earlier.
2. **The planner call** on turns that reach a model (the waterfall's "planner" row); turns the code lanes answer have none.
3. **Speech to text**: two forced Whisper decodes raced; the English one back in about 250-490 ms, the Tamil one 330-1,250 ms; a request waits for the Tamil one (translation check). Measured from the server's telemetry (`data/thunai/telemetry.ndjson`, stt_decode rows of the Phase 0 run).
4. **The end-of-utterance wait** (300 ms of quiet, plus the detector's own lag) and, on console commands, a 326 ms pointer glide that runs BEFORE the step (`Thunai.tsx glide`: 16 + 230 + 80 ms of sleeps).
5. **The wake**: open, "Hi Jarvis" goes through the end-of-utterance wait and one Whisper decode before a tone; closed, through the browser's cloud recogniser, which cannot be run under automation (empty results, then `network`).

Two defects found on the way, both measured: English speech decoded by the forced-Tamil Whisper comes back as a Tamil translation and the translation check refused the right English decode (10 of the 22 English sentences that reached the two-decode race were heard as Tamil, the English decode the surer on all 10), and answers ran 6-35 s of speech.

## What the Phase 0 waterfall says to build first

Levers 1-6 are code-only, free and flagged; they cut the measured biggest pieces (the voice's cold start, the wake path, the STT race's wrong language and wait, the command's glide). Lever 7 (Phase 3) and 8 (Phase 5) shorten answers and keep turns off the planner. Lever 9 picks the planner rung by the bake-off. 10-15 are written up, not built, each for the reason in its row.
