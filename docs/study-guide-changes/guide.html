Chapter 1Rain: three steps, a circle on the map, and proof@@c1@@

In one line

Rain is now three clicks: a past heavy rain → the areas it hit → one area, where the map zooms into a circle and lists its flooded streets one by one, each with Take action and its follow-up, and every figure shows where it came from.

1.1 The three steps (the same on all four tabs)

  1. Past heavy rains, newest first. Each row: the dates, the district average rainfall (for example "27 mm average"), how many streets (or roads, wards, lines) it hit, and the area hit most.
  2. One rain: a "Rain measured" box, then the areas it hit, most first. Each area is a zone (Metro: a line).
  3. One area: the map flies to a dashed circle with the area's name; the panel lists the places one by one. Open one to see why it is listed (the complaints or reports behind it) and its action tracker: assign to a department with a due date and a note, follow what the department reports with photos, verify, and see whether the next comparable rain proved the fix.

Tabs: Flooding, Traffic, Power, Metro. Satellite water seen in the chosen rain is drawn on every tab and named in the map key. Everything that crowded the old panel (three KPIs, insight sentence, "also in this rain" chips, bulk send, repeated "review and send") was removed.

1.2 Proof for every figure

Example (24 Nov rain, read on 29 Nov)

"District average 27 mm from 26 automatic rain gauges (20 inside Chennai); the wettest, Pozhichalur, recorded 68 mm. IMD station: 35 mm." Sources: Chennai Flood Monitor (TN WRD CFM-DSS) and India Meteorological Department, each a link to the official site. An area shows its own rain, for example "29 mm (CFM-DSS gauges, 3 wards)". A street lists its complaints, each with an Open button.

1.3 Only real heavy rains

The pipeline's list of heavy-rain days included days when the gauges recorded almost nothing. 16 November showed "0 mm" with more than 100 flooding reports: the IMD station recorded 0 mm and 25 gauges averaged 0.1 mm (wettest 2 mm). Those were ordinary waterlogging complaints, not a rain.

Why this, not that

A rain is listed only if the gauges did not find it dry: a district average of at least 15 mm, or one gauge at IMD's 64.5 mm heavy-rain mark. Four days were dropped (18 Jul, 28 Aug, 16 Nov, 27 Nov). Showing them would have made the "proof" contradict itself.

1.4 How the circle is drawn

The circle's centre is the average position of the area's places; its radius is the farthest place plus 300 m, between 550 m and 6 km. The camera frames the circle; a place opened from the list or the map gets the camera at street level.

Be honest about

Power-cut hours and train delays in heavy rain are generated demonstration logs (marked as such in the sources). Flooding, satellite water, gauges and traffic records are real. Two September rains had only 6 gauges reporting, so they show "Only 6 gauges reported" instead of an average.

Say it in the review

"Every number on the Rain screen opens to its source: the gauges that measured it, and the complaint that put the street on the list. And we removed days the gauges found dry, so a rain on our screen is a rain."

Chapter 2News: action, proof and only what can be read@@c2@@

In one line

News the AI desk marks Needs action now has an Act button that sends it to the right department officer; every story opens to a full article from a listed outlet; and every source names itself and opens in full.

2.1 Act on news that needs action

  • The department comes from the story's case when it has one, else from the news desk's category (its lead department).
  • The draft names the outlets, what happened and why it needs the department. The Collector reads it, may change it, and approves the send with a password check. Email follows the sandbox rule (the team inbox, not the officer, until live mode).
  • A story on a case is sent on that case: the officer console flags the case and shows the instruction. A news-only story appears in the officer's news card under From the Collector, where the officer writes what was done; the Collector sees that answer on the story ("Department answered: …").
  • A severe case also offers "take action yourself", which opens the incident's own Take action.
Checked end to end (28 Nov)

A Collector sent a solid-waste story; the SWM officer saw it under "From the Collector", marked it done with a note; the Collector's story showed the note.

2.2 One verdict for "Needs action"

The label, the Act button and the "why it matters to you" panel all follow the same AI news-desk verdict. Before, a second AI reading could say "no action needed" on a story labelled "Needs action".

2.3 Only listed outlets, and only full articles

  • News comes only from the outlet allowlist (newspapers such as Daily Thanthi, Dinamalar, The Hindu; TV news; news agencies; government sources). Social media pages are never shown.
  • A news-only story whose every report is only a headline or a search snippet is not listed (30 of 183 news-only stories in the month to 28 Nov). A story linked to an incident stays, because it opens the incident with its complaints and reports.
  • The preview opens on a report that has the whole article; if fetching one fails, it moves to another report of the same story.

2.4 Every source opens in full

  • Incident pop-up: each report in "How it unfolded" has Read the full article (news), On the outlet's site, or Open the incident it was filed as (a merged report); merged incidents open on click; complaint text is shown whole.
  • Differ: under each outlet's claim, Read this article opens that outlet's own report.
  • Developing stories: each step names its source and opens its article.
Say it in the review

"If we show a headline, you can read the article it came from, from a named official outlet. And if a story needs the Collector, one click sends it to the officer who answers for it, and the reply comes back to the same story."

Chapter 3Jarvis, the chatbot and mail@@c3@@

In one line

Jarvis and Ask District IQ now answer with Claude Sonnet, speak with Sarvam's Ritu voice in any browser, can draft a mail to any officer, and handle a few questions in a fixed way so the answer is the same every time.

3.1 Models

SettingBeforeNow
Jarvis's planner (JARVIS_CLAUDE_MODEL)Claude HaikuClaude Sonnet
The shared answer engine for Jarvis and the chatbot (JARVIS_BRAIN_MODEL)Claude HaikuClaude Sonnet
Map Insights (AI_MAP_CLAUDE_MODEL)Claude HaikuClaude Sonnet
Fast steps (routing, drafts)Claude HaikuClaude Haiku (unchanged)

When Claude is unavailable or the site's own spend limit is reached, the gateway falls back to the other configured models (Groq, Bedrock, Gemini). Answers are still checked: every figure must come from a tool result.

3.2 Voice

Jarvis's voice is now the server's Sarvam text-to-speech (model bulbul:v3, speaker Ritu, chosen by the owner from ten samples). Long answers are split into pieces of about 400 characters, read four at a time and joined, about 1.5 to 2 seconds a sentence. The browser's own voices (Edge's Neerja and Pallavi) are only a fallback when the server cannot speak, so Jarvis speaks the same in Chrome and Edge.

3.3 Questions answered in one fixed way

  • "Open incident INC-…" opens the console's own incident pop-up, the one a click on a row opens, with no model.
  • "Which police station is responsible for Avadi?" is always asked of the map as "the nearest police stations to Avadi". The data holds where stations are (OpenStreetMap), not the area each covers, and the answer says so. Avadi, Poonamallee, Tambaram, Pallavaram, Thiruverkadu and Tiruvallur were added as places just outside the district. The answer engine knows Avadi and Ambattur's T-series stations come under the Avadi City Police Commissionerate and Tambaram under Tambaram's (both formed in January 2022).
  • "Jarvis, thank you" gets "You're welcome, Collector." A plain "thank you" within 20 seconds of an answer does too.

3.4 Draft a mail to any officer

From the Menu, the search palette, Jarvis or the chatbot ("draft a mail to the Metrowater officer about …"). The department is guessed from the words; the Collector picks the officer or a staff member from the directory; the AI writes from the Collector's own words only; the Collector edits and approves with a password; it goes under the sandbox rule and reaches the officer console under "From the Collector".

Checked (28 Nov)

"Draft a mail to the Metrowater officer asking for a status report on the sewage overflow complaints in Royapuram by Monday" chose Chennai Metrowater and wrote a formal mail; the same request to Jarvis opened the same composer.

Be honest about

Voice reliability was checked with typed questions; spoken use depends on the microphone and the room. Sonnet costs about 20 times more per answer than Haiku (Chapter 6).

Chapter 4Every console: layout, maps and filters@@c4@@

In one line

Pages scroll as a whole like the Briefing (no tile scrolls inside itself), every map opens on satellite imagery, and the Data Studio, officer console and citizen portal were tidied for the review.

4.1 Page scroll, not tile scroll

One stylesheet (pagescroll.css) makes every page as tall as its content on the Collector and officer consoles. Lists show their full length; long ones show their first rows with "See all". The Overview's map stays in view (sticky) while the cards beside it scroll. Trends shows eight taluks beside the "rising" table with "See all 17 taluks"; the prices card shows eight items with "See all … in full screen".

4.2 Maps

Every console map opens on satellite imagery (the owner's choice, from a screenshot). Rain and Live traffic still switch to the street map while open. "Ask the map" is now called Map Insights on screen, in English and Tamil; spoken commands are unchanged.

4.3 Data Studio

  • One Add data panel across the top: "Upload a file" on the left, Fetch from a link with a large Fetch button on the right; sign-in and schedule one link below.
  • Datasets as cards under it; the open dataset below with only Insights and Alerts. AI reading, Rows, Data card and Data Detective tabs were removed; "Fetch now" and "Last fetched 5 h ago · Hourly" are shown clearly; the 23 column chips fold under one line.

4.4 Department officer console

  • No Jarvis (Ask District IQ stays); opens on Daily; the last tiles (rain and air quality) are no longer cut off.
  • An incident opens as a full centred pop-up like the Collector's, its sections in two columns.
  • A filter that is set (zone, taluk, department on the Collector) shows as a bright white chip.

4.5 Citizen portal

  • The greeting reads Vanakkam, வணக்கம் and the name, one under the other.
  • Accounts are kept. Until 28 Nov evening neither copy of the site saved to AWS, so an account registered on the hosted site lived only until the site slept. The hosted copy now saves (and retries after AWS's "too many requests" answer); an account registered at 00:17 on 29 Nov was confirmed in the AWS store.
Be honest about

Accounts registered on the hosted site before 28 Nov evening were never saved and must be registered again. The ward polygons are a community copy (DataMeet), not GCC-official; the taluk side is official (Chapter 5).

Chapter 5Data: ward to taluk, and the 28 November round@@c5@@

In one line

Each of the 200 GCC wards is now placed in its revenue taluk from official TNGIS revenue-village boundaries, checked village by village against the Chennai Collectorate's list.

5.1 Where the ward to taluk mapping comes from

  1. Ward side: the console's own ward polygons, a DataMeet community copy of the GCC wards (2011-era numbering in several zones). No official ward-to-taluk table is published anywhere.
  2. Taluk side (official): revenue village polygons from TNGIS (Tamil Nadu Geographical Information System, run by TNeGA), public WFS layer tngis_basemap:basemap_revenue_village, each with its taluk.
  3. Checked against the Chennai District Collectorate's list of 17 taluks and their villages (chennai.nic.in/revenue-administration). 136 of 140 TNGIS villages matched the Collectorate's taluk; Kolathur, Peravallur and Siruvallur moved to the Kolathur taluk formed in 2024.
  4. Overlay: each ward is sampled on a 25 m grid; each point is placed in a village; the ward's taluk is the taluk holding the largest share of its area. Shares and flags (a near tie, a ward whose official number lies in another taluk) are kept.

Files: district_intel/reference/ward_taluk.csv (one row per ward), ward_taluk_villages.csv, ward_taluk.meta.json; built by scripts/build_ward_taluk.py. The pipeline only reads the CSV.

Be honest about

TNGIS's terms do not allow publishing derived data without TNeGA's approval, and the CSV is in the repository the hourly GitHub run reads. Mention it if asked about licensing.

5.2 Also in the 28 November commit

  • Duplicate finding with a learned model, and explainable scoring (each case's score shows why).
  • CFM-DSS gauge rain per rain, and satellite flood pictures (Sentinel-1 radar, Sentinel-2 and Landsat optical). Satellite water only counts as a flooded street when it lies on the road; under 20 reporting gauges, no district figure is given.
  • Area-wise rain actions with a tracker: withdraw, move, and a rain floor of 20 mm (ward or district) or 64.5 mm (wettest gauge) before a later rain can test a fix.
  • Case lifecycle: reopen, and generated resolutions for the demonstration data (hidden from the Collector's audit view).
  • Officer, citizen and Manu Desk consoles in the Collector's look; Jarvis compare dashboards; history search.

Chapter 6Hosting, saving and what it costs@@c6@@

In one line

The public site runs on Modal as one container; it is the one copy that saves to AWS; the 27 November topic guard keeps every assistant on the district's work.

6.1 Hosting

  • One container (at most one, so the store is saved from one place), 1 to 4 CPU cores (2 to 4 in demo mode) and 4 to 12 GB of memory. Demo mode keeps it running all the time and never preempted; normal mode sleeps after 30 minutes with no visitors.
  • The hosted copy is the only one that saves to AWS; this PC's copy reads only. Two savers would give new rows the same ids.

Be honest about

The spend records include our own testing: 4,369 of the recorded Claude calls were automated evaluation runs, not real use. The AWS bill (S3, DynamoDB, API Gateway, Lambda) was not read for this guide. Groq, Gemini and Sarvam were used within their free tiers.

6.3 Topic guard (27 Nov)

Jarvis, Ask District IQ, the officer chat, Map Insights and the Studio analyst answer only about the district's records, the console and District IQ. A plainly off-topic question (sports, films, jokes, coding) gets one fixed sentence in English, Tamil or Tanglish, with no model call.
